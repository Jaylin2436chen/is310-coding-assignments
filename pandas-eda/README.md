# National Park Visits EDA

I decided to explore the annual recreation visits for U.S. national parks. I used Pandas to explore the dataset and Altair to make an interactive visualization to compare visits at the top five most visited parks. In the chart you can hover over each chart points to see park name, year, and total visit count.
