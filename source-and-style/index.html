Howl's Moving Castle

Description

Howl's Moving Castle is a Japanese animated fantasy movie directed and created by Hayao Miyazaki. The movie follows Sophie who is a young woman who is soon transformed into an elderly woman by a curse put on her. She then later enters the moving castle of a wizard named Howl.

This movie I chose relates to ethics and discrimination because Sophie is treated differently due to her age and appearance compared to earlier when she was younger. It also shows the effects of war, the misuse of political power, and how important it is to look beyond a person's appearance.

Ethics and Discrimination

The movie challenges discrimination based on age, physical appearance, and social status. Sophie's transformation allows the story to show how assumptions about a person's appearance can affect how that person is treated. The movie also questions the ethics of war by showing how political leaders place ordinary people in danger.
