# Harry Potter Film Wiki Page

## Background:
The webpage that I decided to scrape is Wikipedia because it is a wide community edited wiki that has an overview page about all of the Harry Potter movie series and different articles for each movie. I am scraping all nine (main page and the 8 movies) pages and am recording each page’s word count and its number of internal links.

## My Research Question:
How does the word count and internal links count differ across all of the eight Harry Potter movie article's Wikipedia? 

The data I collected can be useful to researchers exploring how online communities document different popular movies and series like Harry Potter. Additionally, the overall counts will show the differences in article coverage.

## Limitations:
While the ability to collect all words and internal links can be very useful, there are limitations within it, such as how it may collect words from tables and references that could possibly be skipped. Additionally, as the web scraping script is collecting internal links throughout all the pages, it may collect repeated linked if there are mutiple that lead to the same place making it a bit inaccurate.
